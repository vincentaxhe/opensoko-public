Thanks for playing OpenSoko! For help with the app, your purchase, or anything else, email mhe1989@163.com.

Restore your purchase

OpenSoko offers one optional unlock (Unlimited Solver). If it is missing after you reinstall the app or move to a new device, open OpenSoko and tap Menu → Restore Purchases. If that does not help, email us above and we will sort it out.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 7, 2026  |  App: OpenSoko  |  Developer: Vincent He

OpenSoko does not collect any personal data. OpenSoko is an offline puzzle game. It requires no account, no internet connection, and has no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress, completed levels, achievements, settings, and any level packs you import are stored only on your device in a local database. This data is never transmitted to us or to any server, and we have no way to access it.

In-App Purchases

OpenSoko offers one optional, one-time In-App Purchase that unlocks unlimited use of the built-in puzzle solver. All purchases are processed and handled entirely by Apple through the App Store, subject to Apple’s own Privacy Policy and Terms. We never receive or store your payment or account information.

No data collection

Because OpenSoko collects no personal data, there is nothing to share, sell, or use for advertising or tracking. The app works fully offline.

Deleting your data

All data is local to your device. You can delete it at any time by deleting OpenSoko from your device (or by reinstalling the app).

Children

OpenSoko does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
